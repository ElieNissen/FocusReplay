# FocusReplay

## Register

product

## Users

People working on a Windows PC who struggle to start and stay focused. Starting a session must require one click, with no goal, title, activity log or classification to fill in. French is the initial interface language.

## Product Purpose

Make work habits visible through private periodic screenshots, estimated foreground application time, and an interactive, scrubbable replay. Help people understand their day without pretending screenshots prove productivity. A local MP3 can mark the beginning of a session; an MP4 preserves a chosen day or session.

## Brand Personality

Observant, calm, practical. A personal mirror rather than a judge. No policing, shame, AI scores, streak pressure or mandatory input. Optional personal reward budgets support small breaks and larger leisure rewards without restricting ordinary pauses.

## Anti-references

Avoid surveillance control rooms, competitive productivity scores, generic dashboard card grids, cloud-account onboarding and compulsory objective forms. Camera use must be optional, clearly consented and visible. A picture cannot prove absence from a desk.

## Design Principles

- Start first: a single primary action with sensible persistent settings.
- Let the evidence speak: exact capture timestamps, explicit gaps, application estimates labeled as estimates.
- Review whenever: dragging the timeline updates the preview while capture continues independently.
- Private by construction: screenshots, audio and activity live outside source control, no analytics or remote fonts.
- Bound the footprint: compression, expiry and storage cap.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

System typography, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, labels beyond color, resizable desktop layout and keyboard transport controls.
